🗣 오늘의 영어로 · 쉬운 현대 영어

In the beginning, the Word already existed. The Word was with God, and the Word was God. He was with God from the very beginning. Everything was made through him; nothing was made without him. In him was life, and that life was the light of all people. The light shines in the darkness, and the darkness has not put it out. There was a man sent from God, whose name was John. He came as a witness to speak about the Light, so that everyone might believe through him.

오늘의 미션

🍯 말씀 한 스푼

아무리 짙은 어둠도 작은 빛 하나를 이기지 못합니다. 오늘 당신의 하루에도 그 life(생명)와 light(빛)이 비치고 있어요. 낙심되는 날, 이 한 문장을 영어로 소리 내어 읽어 보세요 — the darkness comprehended it not.

✏️ 나눔 — 요즘 내 삶에서 ‘빛’이 되어 준 순간이나 사람은 누구였나요?
